## Overall Business Context (May–June 2026)

Racing is tracking **below plan and below prior year** across all top-line metrics:
- Handle consistently **-7% to -12% YoY**, and **-8% to -11% vs Q1 forecast**
- GGR tracking similarly, **-7% to -12% YoY**
- Market share holding at **~16–17%** (modest erosion of ~0.1–0.5pp YoY)
- New-to-racing (NTG) activations severely depressed: **-50% to -63% YoY**
- Cross-sell from sportsbook also down sharply: **-42% to -58% YoY**

**Key headwinds noted in reports:**
- P1 platform incidents (one week cost ~$200K in lost handle; Derby week impacted by P1)
- Race cancellations/schedule changes (cost $2.9M in comparable handle one week)
- Florida (non-SBK state) continuing to trend weaker
- Decline in low-value SBK state actives dragging overall active count

## How to Use This Data in Business Cases

**When estimating handle impact of a feature:**
- Baseline non-event week handle: ~$42–49M (TVG + FDR combined)
- FDR share of handle: ~34% (~$16–17M/week)
- GGR margin: ~7.5–8% of handle
- 1% handle lift on FDR = ~$160–170K/week = ~$8–9M/year

**When estimating active user impact:**
- Total weekly actives: ~97–102K
- FDR actives: ~46–47K
- SBK cross-play cohort (most valuable): ~20–24K actives, $266–$391 AHPU

**When referencing YoY trends:**
- Current trend is -7% to -12% YoY on handle — any feature that stabilizes or reverses this has significant value
- FDR specifically is bucking the trend (+19% handle YoY) — FDR-specific improvements have strong tailwinds

**Conversion / activation context:**
- NTG activations are severely depressed (-50 to -63% YoY) — new user features have high strategic value
- Cross-sell rate from SBK is ~1–2%; even small improvements here are material given 1M+ SBK actives
